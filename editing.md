# Intros
- Match title, thumbnail and Intros
- Get expectations from title and thumbnail satisfied in the intro
- Show the problem and voiceover it
- Make pacing match the style
- Destroy expectations from title and thumbnails after first five seconds of meeting them

# Editing
## Cuts
Full-screen transitions (with whoosh maybe) from one scene to another

### A-Roll
- You can see and hear the subject (talking with a camera for example)
- Personal

Use it for **short** periods of time when saying something **important**

### B-Roll
- Recorded separately from audio
- Viewers can see what I'm talking about
- More interesting than A-Roll

Use it as much as possible

### Motion graphics (Variant of B-Roll)
Example: Animation
- Fast
- Simple to understand

Useful for explanations of complex things

## Engagement Spice
### Movement
- Make it move slowly
- Change perspective
- Zoom in to draw attention
- Wiggle (+-)

### Captions
- Not the best choice when used just to fill up space
- Make viewer pay attention to what I'm saying
- 3 or less words at a time

### Highlight
- Animate important parts being highlighted
- Or make it glow

### Darken/Blur
- Darken/blur everything except part that you want to highlight

### Shif hue

### Visuals
- Circles
- Arrows
- Underlines

### Effects
- Color grading
- Vinette
- Particles

## Visual continuity
### Appearance/Disappearance
- Animate moving/scaling/fading in/out
- Make it appear/disappear suddenly, but with a sound effect

### Cuts
- Make sure viewer's focus is in the same place

Break it every now and then

# Audio
## SFX
- Woosh when something moves
- Highlight when something is highlighted
- Pop when something appears/disappears
- Risers: Build tension, something important is about to happen
- Hits: Release tension, emphesize moments
- Drone: Make feeling of mystery and intrigue; Darker moods, suspection

## Music
- Anticipating: When about to say something important
- Inovative: Exciting, when something is grownd breaking

Split the video by subjects.
Decide the mood for each subject and pick the song

Tips:
- Pause abruptly to jolt the viewer
- Lack of music brings attention to other parts of the video
- Slowly fade out when current segment is about to end
- Sync hits and dips
